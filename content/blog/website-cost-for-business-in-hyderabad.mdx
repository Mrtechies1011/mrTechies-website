## How Much Does a Website Cost for a Business in Hyderabad? (Direct Answer)

A business website in Hyderabad typically costs between **₹15,000 and ₹60,000** in 2026, depending on design quality, SEO readiness, number of pages, and performance requirements.

Custom or growth‑focused websites may cost more.

## What Factors Affect Website Cost in Hyderabad?

Website pricing is not random. Costs depend on:

- Number of pages
- Custom vs template design
- SEO requirements
- Website speed & performance
- Content creation
- Integrations (WhatsApp, booking, CRM)

The more your website supports **business growth**, the higher the value.
